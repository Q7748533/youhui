Privacy

Privacy policy.

Cloud Storage Deals is a public price index. It has no accounts, no sign-up forms and no user profiles. This page explains the small amount of data that passes through the site, and what changes when analytics or advertising is enabled.

At a glance
  • No accounts, no forms, no newsletter
  • No advertising or analytics cookies before you consent
  • No profile of you is built or sold
  • Data requests: privacy@example.com

Effective date: October 21, 2026. This version replaces any earlier policy.

Who is responsible

Cloud Storage Deals ("we", "us") publishes an independent index of publicly listed cloud storage prices. For the purposes of the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation, we are the controller of the limited personal data described below. You can reach us at privacy@example.com.

What we do not collect

  • No account data. There is no registration, login, checkout or payment on this site.
  • No form data. The site has no contact form, comment field or newsletter signup.
  • No profile. We do not build user profiles, run remarketing audiences of our own, or sell personal information.
  • No content uploads. We never ask you for files, and nothing you store with a provider is visible to us.

Information processed when you visit

Like any website, the pages you request are served by a hosting provider. Our site is delivered through Cloudflare, which processes technical request data — such as IP address, requested URL, referrer, browser type and timestamp — to route traffic, serve cached pages and block abuse. This data is used for delivery and security only, not to identify individuals, and we do not combine it with any other information.

Cloudflare acts as our hosting and security provider. Its own handling of this data is described in the Cloudflare privacy policy.

Cookies and similar technologies

The site itself sets no cookies. Cookies appear only where analytics or advertising is enabled, and only after you give consent where consent is required. The table lists what can be set, and by whom.

Analytics

Where analytics is enabled, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages are useful and how visitors arrive. It sets first-party cookies named _ga and _ga_<container-id>, which hold a randomly generated client identifier. Reports we read are aggregated: page views, referrers, country and device category. We do not use Analytics to identify you, and we do not enable advertising personalization or Google Signals in Analytics. User-level data is kept for the shortest retention period Google offers, and IP addresses are not used to build profiles.

Advertising

Where advertising is enabled, third-party vendors including Google use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info (US) or Your Online Choices (EU). Opting out does not remove ads; it makes them less relevant.

Legal bases

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — for analytics and advertising cookies. Nothing in these categories loads before you agree, and you can withdraw at any time with effect for the future.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — for serving pages, keeping the site available and preventing abuse, which is limited to the technical request data described above.

Service providers and sharing

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising unless you consent. Two processors are involved in running the site: Cloudflare (hosting, caching and security) and, where enabled, Google (analytics and advertising). Both are bound by their own data processing terms. Where data is transferred outside the EEA or UK, the transfer relies on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent safeguard offered by the provider.

How long data is kept

Technical request logs at the hosting layer are retained for a short operational window and then discarded. Analytics data is kept for the shortest retention period Google offers. Advertising data is retained according to the settings of the advertising account. We keep no separate database of visitors.

Your rights and choices

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland you may request access to your personal data, correction, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. If you are a California resident, you may request to know, delete or correct personal information, and to opt out of sale or sharing; we do not sell personal information.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@example.com. We answer within 30 days and may ask for enough detail to locate the data involved. You also have the right to complain to your local supervisory authority.

Children

This site is a price index aimed at adults making purchasing decisions. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16.

Links to other sites

Every provider page links to the provider's own website. Once you follow that link you are on their site, and their privacy policy and cookie practices apply. We have no control over, and no visibility into, what they collect.

Changes to this policy

If the way the site handles data changes — for example when analytics or advertising is switched on — this page is updated and the effective date above changes. Material changes are reflected here before the new processing starts.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about your data: privacy@example.com. For questions about a provider's plans, billing or account, contact that provider directly — we do not sell storage plans or process payments.
