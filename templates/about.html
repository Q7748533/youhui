What this site does

Official sources

Prices are read from each provider's own public pricing page. The source link is shown with every provider and offer.

Conservative records

When a current price cannot be verified, the site leaves it empty. It does not estimate or carry over an older value.

Regular refreshes

An automated pipeline checks selected public pricing pages on a fixed schedule and records when each result was last checked.

What the labels mean

  • Verified means a current public price was readable on the official source during the latest fetch.
  • No verified price means the page was reachable, but a current public price could not be confirmed.
  • Source unavailable means the source could not be fetched or is not available to the public pipeline.
  • Expired means the published validity date has passed and the record should not be treated as current.

How pricing data is collected

Disclosure: every figure on this site is collected by an automated pipeline, not by human editors. The pipeline fetches each provider's official public pricing page on a fixed schedule, extracts only plans and prices that are publicly displayed there, and records the exact time of every check. Automation is used to keep prices current and verifiable — never to invent, estimate or embellish them.

The pipeline tracks public pricing pages from selected cloud storage providers. It checks each source on a regular schedule and records only plans and prices that can be verified on the provider's official page.

Prices that are unavailable, outdated, struck through or otherwise unclear are not estimated or carried over from an earlier check. Each offer page links back to its official source and shows when the data was last checked.
