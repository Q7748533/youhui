About site maintenance.

Cloud Storage Deals is maintained as an independent public pricing index. The site owner reviews the provider list, data rules and generated pages.

Maintenance policy

Provider sources and site content are maintained directly by the project owner. This page does not provide a public issue-submission or support channel.

What is maintained

  • Provider sources: the list of tracked cloud storage providers and their official pricing URLs.
  • Data rules: prices are shown only when they can be verified on an official public page.
  • Generated pages: provider pages, offer records, comparison pages, sitemap and related metadata.
  • Refresh schedule: the public data pipeline checks configured sources on a regular schedule.

For account, billing or plan questions, contact the relevant provider through its official website. This project does not sell storage plans or process payments.
